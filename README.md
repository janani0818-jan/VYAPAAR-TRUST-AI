# VyapaarTrust AI 🚀

### Explainable Financial Intelligence for MSMEs

VyapaarTrust AI is an AI-powered financial intelligence platform designed to help Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) understand their financial health, identify risk areas, and take better business decisions.

Instead of providing only raw financial data, VyapaarTrust AI converts financial indicators into an easy-to-understand **TrustScore**, explains the factors behind the score, and provides actionable recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

MSMEs generate large amounts of financial information through sales, invoices, payments, receivables, cash flow and compliance activities.

However, many small businesses face difficulties in:

- Understanding their overall financial health
- Identifying financial risk early
- Monitoring cash-flow stability
- Understanding payment and receivables behavior
- Converting financial data into actionable decisions
- Preparing themselves for better financial opportunities

Traditional accounting software mainly focuses on recording and managing transactions.

VyapaarTrust AI focuses on the next layer:

> **Turning financial data into understanding and actionable business decisions.**

---

## 💡 Our Solution

VyapaarTrust AI evaluates MSME financial health across multiple dimensions and generates an explainable TrustScore between **0 and 100**.

The platform follows:

**Financial Data → Analysis → TrustScore → Explanation → Recommendation → Action**

The system is designed to help an MSME owner understand:

- How healthy their business is
- Why their score is high or low
- Which factors are affecting their financial health
- What areas need improvement
- How potential improvements could affect their financial position

---

## ⭐ Key Features

### 1. TrustScore

A simplified financial-health score from **0–100** that summarizes important business indicators.

### 2. Explainable Scoring

Instead of presenting a black-box score, the system shows the major factors contributing to the result.

### 3. Financial Health Analysis

The platform evaluates important areas such as:

- Cash-flow health
- Payment and receivables behavior
- Financial consistency
- Compliance and documentation
- Business/revenue stability

### 4. Actionable Recommendations

The system converts identified weaknesses into practical improvement suggestions.

### 5. What-If Analysis

Users can explore how changes in financial conditions could potentially influence their financial-health outcome.

### 6. MSME-Focused Dashboard

The interface is designed to present complex financial information in a simple and understandable format.

### 7. AI-Assisted Intelligence

AI capabilities can support financial interpretation, natural-language explanations and personalized recommendations while keeping core financial calculations structured and explainable.

📊 TrustScore Methodology

VyapaarTrust AI is designed around multiple financial-health dimensions rather than relying on a single financial metric.

The major dimensions are:

Dimension	Purpose
Cash-Flow Health	Evaluates liquidity and cash-flow condition
Payment / Receivables Behavior	Identifies payment and collection patterns
Financial Consistency	Evaluates stability and consistency
Compliance / Documentation	Considers documentation and compliance-related signals
Business / Revenue Stability	Evaluates revenue and business stability

The financial indicators can be normalized to a common scale and combined using a weighted scoring methodology.

The objective is to make the resulting TrustScore:

Understandable
Consistent
Explainable
Actionable

🤖 Role of AI

AI is used as an intelligence layer rather than replacing the complete financial scoring mechanism.

The system can use AI capabilities for:

Understanding financial patterns
Generating natural-language explanations
Producing personalized recommendations
Supporting conversational interaction
Assisting users in interpreting financial information

The core financial scoring logic should remain structured, deterministic, and auditable.

This separation helps reduce the risk of blindly relying on generative AI for important financial calculations.

🧪 Validation Methodology

The prototype can be evaluated using structured financial scenarios and controlled test cases.

The purpose of validation is to check whether the system behaves logically when important financial conditions change.

Test Case 1 — Healthy Business
Scenario

A business has:

Stable revenue
Healthy cash flow
Good payment behavior
Consistent financial activity
Proper documentation
Expected Result

The business should receive a relatively strong financial-health outcome.

Test Case 2 — High Receivables
Scenario

A business has increasing outstanding customer payments and delayed collections.

Expected Result

The receivables/payment-related indicators should deteriorate and influence the overall TrustScore.

Test Case 3 — Unstable Cash Flow
Scenario

A business experiences significant fluctuations in incoming and outgoing cash.

Expected Result

The cash-flow stability component should indicate increased financial pressure.

Test Case 4 — Data / Compliance Issues
Scenario

A business has incomplete, inconsistent, or insufficient documentation.

Expected Result

The system should identify the relevant issue and provide an appropriate warning or recommendation.

Test Case 5 — Improvement Scenario
Scenario

A business improves one of its weaker financial areas.

Expected Result

The system should demonstrate how the improvement could positively influence the relevant financial-health indicators and overall outcome.

JWT_SECRET

Create a local .env file when required.

Example:

JWT_SECRET=your_secret_here

Do not upload .env to GitHub.
